# Felipe Rojas — Professional Profile

Senior IT Technical Support Analyst with more than ten years of IT support
experience, including County of Los Angeles service beginning in 2015 and
service in the current senior role beginning September 2022. Provides
advanced, largely independent technical support for hardware, software,
application, account, device, printer, and network issues, escalating only
the most complex cases, and provides lead direction to lower-level support
staff. Administers identity, endpoint, Microsoft 365, and asset-related
systems within a defined operational scope, and has contributed to internal
application development, workflow automation, technical documentation, and
end-user training. Coordinates with vendors, stakeholders, developers,
system administrators, and County ISD staff on project and incident work.
Provides bilingual (English/Spanish) support. Prior related roles: IT
Technical Support Analyst I, Intermediate Clerk, Veteran Intern, and
technical support at a private college.
