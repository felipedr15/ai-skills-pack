# Research Notes Knowledge

## Purpose
Capture reusable, reviewed lessons and references about research notes.

## What Belongs Here
Concise, sourced patterns, lessons, and examples with durable value.

## What Does Not Belong Here
Secrets, personal data, unverified claims, project-only temporary notes, or binding standards.

## Naming Guidance
Use lowercase hyphenated topic filenames; include dates only when time context matters.
