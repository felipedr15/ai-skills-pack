## Support and Diagnostics

Troubleshoots, diagnoses, resolves, and documents difficult hardware,
software, application, account, device, printer, and network connectivity
problems, escalating only the most complex cases to other technical staff
or vendors.

## Lead Direction and Incident Coordination

Provides lead direction and guidance to lower-level technical support
staff. Coordinates resolution of major hardware, software, application, and
deployment incidents with end users, vendors, and technical staff.

## Systems and Identity Administration

Installs, configures, tests, and maintains specialized business software
and endpoint configurations. Administers identity, account, mailbox, and
licensing access within a defined operational scope. Analyzes and improves
hardware and software performance through upgrades and configuration
changes.

## Documentation and Standards

Develops installation procedures, technical documentation, and
quality-assurance standards. Reviews ticket and incident trends and
implements workflow improvements. Develops and conducts end-user and
technical-staff training. Recommends hardware and software standards and
prepares procurement specifications.

## Asset and Procurement

Supports software distribution, endpoint configuration, and large-scale
deployment projects. Leads the annual IT asset inventory, tracking, and
equipment lifecycle coordination.

## Project and Vendor Coordination

Coordinates deployments, migrations, office relocations, and
vendor-delivered projects. Prepares project plans, schedules, status
reports, and executive-facing summaries. Collaborates with employees,
managers, executives, vendors, developers, system administrators, and
County ISD staff.

## Security and Compliance (Scoped)

Supports localized information-security and compliance procedures within
assigned scope.
