Evidence tiers, from strongest to weakest:

- **Direct** — named in the professional resume and/or the senior job
  description.
- **Project-evidenced** — demonstrated through a specific, named project.
- **Skills-list only** — named in a platform/skills list without deeper
  project or administration detail.
- **Draft-matrix only** — appears only in an internal, proposed roles/
  ownership matrix; treated as an unconfirmed, proposed assignment, never as
  a finalized fact, and excluded from expertise entries entirely.
- **Not evidenced** — considered but no supporting reference was found;
  omitted from `felipe-rojas.expertise.json`.
